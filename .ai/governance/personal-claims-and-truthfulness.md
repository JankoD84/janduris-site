# Personal Claims and Truthfulness

## Scope

Applies to claims about the owner, including:

- job history
- role titles
- employment dates
- employers
- responsibilities
- education and training
- years of experience
- skill levels
- languages
- technical proficiency
- achievements
- certifications
- clients/customers
- professional positioning

## Required Authority

A personal claim requires one of:

1. current approved site content
2. explicit owner-provided fact for the task
3. authoritative source supplied for the task

Do not expand beyond the approved wording.

## Forbidden Inference

Do not infer stronger claims from weak signals:

- `uses Playwright` does not imply `expert Playwright engineer`
- `built a prototype` does not imply `production platform architect`
- `worked with a technology` does not imply expert proficiency
- `project exists` does not imply commercial success
- `repository exists` does not prove professional expertise

## Tone and Marketing

Marketing/positioning may be polished, but it must not invent or strengthen factual claims. If a claim has reputational consequences, keep it close to the source.

## Metadata

Titles, descriptions, Open Graph text, social previews, and structured data must follow the same personal-claim rules as visible page copy.
